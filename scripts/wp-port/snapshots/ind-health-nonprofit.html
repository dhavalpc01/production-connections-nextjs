Industries We Serve

Medical practices and nonprofits. Nobody chooses either one on price, and nobody chooses either one quickly. They choose the one that feels safe.

Inside This Group

Three Industries. The Same Currency.

A patient picking a practice and a donor picking a cause are asking the same question in different words: can I believe these people. Everything else follows from the answer.

Medical Practices

Medical practice marketing is about earning patient trust before they ever call.

Production Connections comes to meet you in person, showcases your doctors and team through authentic video, and helps drive qualified appointments.

We’re a marketing and video agency, and it all comes from one team.

Nonprofits

Nonprofit marketing is about telling meaningful stories that inspire people to donate, volunteer and support your mission.

Production Connections comes to meet you in person, learns your mission, captures your impact through video, and helps generate awareness and support.

We’re a marketing and video agency, and it all comes from one team.

E-learning & Education

E-learning and education marketing starts from a prospect who can find a thousand free videos promising the same transformation — and who cannot tell a program that genuinely works from the countdown-timer crowd that only says so.

Production Connections comes to learn your program and your learner, puts your teaching on camera where a skeptic can see it, and builds the search, brand and follow-up that turn belief into enrollments people actually finish.

We’re a marketing and video agency, and it all comes from one team.
