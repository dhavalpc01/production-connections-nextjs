The Reality Check

A handful of strangers’ reviews are doing your marketing for you.

Here’s how the one big trip of the year actually gets chosen. Somebody sees something in February. A video, a photo, a friend’s post. They save it. They think about it for weeks. They show their partner. They start imagining themselves there.

By the time a date goes into a booking box in June, the decision is basically made. What happens after that is confirmation, not consideration.

You are nowhere in February. Your entire marketing budget fires in June, at people already comparing prices, which is the one moment where you have no advantage at all and price is the only lever left.

Then comes the confirmation phase, and that’s where the second problem lives. They don’t read your website. They scroll straight past it to the reviews and hand the whole decision to a jury of strangers, half of whom are still furious about a thermostat from eighteen months ago.

your happiest guests would out-sell any ad you ever ran, they just need a little shove to open their mouths

Here’s what we do about it. We get you into the dreaming months with video and content people save and share, then own the confirmation phase with reviews and search. Then we make damn sure they book on your site, not somebody else’s.

A Two-Star Tantrum

One guest gets sunburned, sulks through breakfast, and fires off a two-star tantrum about a slow omelet. That tantrum now stands at your front door like a bouncer waving future bookers away.

Not One Damn Word Online

Your happy guests exist. They left misty-eyed because the trip was perfect. Then they drove home and said not one damn word online, while the cranky one sat down and wrote a novel.

Built By Somebody Else

So the dream gets built by somebody else, and the confirmation gets written by whoever woke up angriest that week. And an aggregator takes fifteen to twenty percent for handing you a guest who was practically already yours.

FAQ

Travel Marketing FAQs

01What does hospitality and travel marketing involve?

Travel marketing means reaching people during the months they’re dreaming about a trip, then winning the review-driven confirmation phase and capturing the booking direct. Production Connections is a travel marketing agency that runs video, search, AI answers, social, reviews, and paid from one team after staying at your property.

02Why does video matter so much for a travel business?

Because travel is a feeling somebody sits with for months, and video is the only thing that lets them feel your place before arriving. It builds the desire a thumbnail and a rate never will. Most properties lean on stock-style photos, which leaves a real damn opening.

03How do you help us depend less on Booking, Expedia, and other aggregators?

By building demand that exists before somebody opens an aggregator. Hospitality and travel marketing breaks the commission cycle when travelers already know your name, your site closes cleanly, and your email brings past guests back without a middleman in between.

04How do you handle our online reviews and reputation?

With a system that asks your happiest guests at the right moment, responds properly to the rough ones, and surfaces the proof where travelers check. Reviews decide more travel purchases than anything you say about yourself, so leaving them to chance is expensive.

05How do you fill slow seasons without slashing rates?

By marketing to past guests and nearby markets with reasons to come rather than discounts, and by starting earlier so the shoulder season fills before you panic. Tourism marketing built only on price teaches travelers to wait for your next drop.

06Do you really meet clients in person?

Yes, and for a travel business it matters most of all. We can’t capture the feeling of your place or target the right travelers until we’ve actually experienced it. That comes from being there, not guessing from afar.
