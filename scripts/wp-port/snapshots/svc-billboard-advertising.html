The Reality Check

A driver gets three seconds with your billboard, tops

They’re doing 65, sipping coffee, half-listening to a podcast, eyes mostly on the road. Your board swings into view and back out of it in the time it takes to blink twice.

In those three seconds you either land or you’re gone for good.

So what do most businesses cram up there? A phone number, a web address, a tagline, a logo, and eleven words jammed edge to edge like a ransom note. Nobody reads a single line of it. The driver’s already three exits down the highway.

It doesn’t have to go like that. We treat a billboard as a three-second design problem and a location problem, then solve both. That’s the whole job, and almost nobody does it.
